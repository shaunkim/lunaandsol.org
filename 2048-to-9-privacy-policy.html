2048⁹ Privacy Policy

2048⁹ (pronounced “2048-to-9”) is an iPhone and iPad game published by Luna and Sol. You can play offline and without a Luna and Sol account.

Gameplay and local storage

Your current game, best score, and gameplay hint are stored on your device. Luna and Sol does not operate a game server.

Advertising and privacy choices

When ads are enabled, Google AdMob can display non-personalized banner ads. The app checks Google’s consent settings before requesting ads and shows a privacy message when required. When Google requires a way to revisit your choices, open Privacy Choices in the game menu. You can continue playing if ads are unavailable.

Google may process information such as IP address, device information, ad interactions, and diagnostics to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. The app does not request access to Apple’s advertising identifier. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy.

Apple Game Center

If you open Leaderboard and sign in to Game Center, the app sends your best score to Apple for ranking. Your Game Center nickname and score may appear to other players according to your settings. Apple handles sign-in and leaderboard data under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases, sharing, and ratings

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. A verified purchase stops banner ad requests. Use Restore Purchases in the game menu to check for an existing purchase. If you share a score card, you choose where to send it using the iOS share sheet. Rate opens Apple’s review prompt when iOS makes it available.
