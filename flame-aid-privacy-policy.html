Flame Aid for Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Applies to Flame Aid for Android, version 1.0

In short: Flame Aid calculates on your device. It has no user accounts, ads, analytics, or online calculation service. Your inputs, results, and calculation log stay on your device.

Who publishes Flame Aid

Flame Aid is published by Luna and Sol. For privacy questions, contact hello@lunaandsol.org.

Information the app handles

Flame Aid processes your fuel selections, calculation inputs, and results on your device. It does not send these items to Luna and Sol or to a Flame Aid server. Calculation history and app preferences are stored in the app's private local storage.

You can clear the calculation log in the app. Uninstalling Flame Aid removes its app-private local data.

Clipboard

If you choose Copy in the calculation log, Flame Aid places the exported CSV text on your device's clipboard. Android and your device control clipboard access and retention. You decide whether to paste or share that text elsewhere.

Network access, advertising, and analytics

The Android app does not request internet access and does not include advertising or analytics functionality. Calculations work offline. If you open a link such as this policy, your browser connects to the website hosting the page; that browser and network connection are subject to their own providers' privacy practices.

Sharing and retention

Luna and Sol does not receive or retain your calculation inputs, results, or local log. These remain on your device unless you choose to copy or share them using Android features. Any app or service you choose to share information with handles it under its own privacy policy.

Children

Flame Aid is a combustion engineering calculator and is not designed specifically for children. The app does not collect personal information from users.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how it handles information, this policy will be updated to describe those practices.
