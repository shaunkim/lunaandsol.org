2048³ for Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Applies to Android version 1.3.0 and later

In short: 2048³ does not ask you to create a Luna and Sol account and does not use advertising in Android version 1.3.0. Your game board and local settings stay on your device. If you use Google Play Games, Google processes your Play Games identity, game scores, and service diagnostics to provide sign-in and leaderboard features.

Who we are

2048³ is published by Luna and Sol. For privacy questions, contact hello@lunaandsol.org.

Information the app handles

Luna and Sol does not receive your personal information or game data through 2048³. Your scores, best score, game state, milestone records, and appearance preference are stored locally on your device. They remain there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Android version 1.3.0 uses Google Play Games Services for its leaderboards. If you use that service, Google Play Games processes your gamer identity (including your gamer name and profile avatar), game scores submitted to the leaderboards, and service analytics and diagnostics. The app submits your best total score and the score recorded when you first reach the 2048 milestone on one, two, or three faces. The game does not read your email address or friends list. Google encrypts Play Games data in transit using HTTPS. Google handles this information under its own terms and privacy policy; you can manage or delete Play Games data through your Play Games profile or Google Account.

The app does not request access to your device's location, contacts, or photo library. The Google Play Games profile avatar described above is provided by Google Play Games, not read from your device's photos. The app does not use advertising or an advertising ID in version 1.3.0.

Sharing a score

If you choose Share, Android opens its system share sheet with your score text. You choose whether and where to send it. The selected app or service then handles the information under its own privacy policy; 2048³ does not send the score to a server.

Network access

When Google Play Games is available, the app sends the leaderboard scores described above to Google Play Games over an encrypted connection. This is optional platform functionality: you can continue playing locally without opening a leaderboard. Opening a link or sharing content is initiated by you and may involve services governed by their own policies.

Children

2048³ does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

If a later version adds advertising, purchases, or other data practices, this policy will be updated to describe them before that version is distributed.
