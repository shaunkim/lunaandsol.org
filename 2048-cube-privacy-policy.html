2048³ for iOS and Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026 · Applies to iOS 1.4.0 and Android 1.3.0

In short: iOS 1.4.0 is designed to show banner ads and occasional interstitial ads through Unity LevelPlay and Meta Audience Network. You can buy Ad Free once to remove ads. Ads are requested only when the app's consent and privacy checks allow them. Android 1.3.0 does not include ads or the Ad Free purchase. Android 1.3.0 uses Google Play Games for optional leaderboards.

Who we are

2048³ is published by Luna and Sol. For privacy questions, contact hello@lunaandsol.org.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, best score, game state, and appearance preference are stored locally on your device. The app does not send this gameplay information to Luna and Sol or use it to select ads. It remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

If you buy Ad Free, the App Store processes the payment. The app receives the purchase entitlement needed to remove ads and can check it again when you restore purchases. Luna and Sol does not receive your payment card or billing information.

Advertising and information used by ad partners

On iOS 1.4.0, Unity LevelPlay mediates ads from Meta Audience Network. Their SDKs may process device identifiers (including the advertising identifier only when Apple permits access), advertising data such as ad delivery, impressions and taps, and limited technical data needed to deliver, measure, secure and troubleshoot ads. This includes ad-service performance data and SDK crash or diagnostic reports. The game does not send scores, moves, saved game state or other gameplay activity for ad targeting, and Luna and Sol does not run separate game-performance or user-behavior analytics.

Meta's SDK privacy manifest declares device identifiers and advertising data for third-party advertising and tracking, and unlinked crash and diagnostic data for analytics. Unity's LevelPlay privacy questionnaire identifies device identifiers for tracking, advertising data, and ad-service performance data. This app does not send gameplay moves, scores, or saved game activity to ad partners; ad impressions and taps are described as advertising data. Ad partners may associate ad events and technical data with a device or advertising identifier as described in their own privacy disclosures. Luna and Sol does not receive individual advertising identifiers or raw ad-request records through the app, though partners may provide aggregated advertising reports.

On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission controls access to the advertising identifier for tracking. If you decline, the app and its partners must not use the IDFA for tracking; you can change this choice in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads may be limited or unavailable. Where local law requires additional ad consent, it must be obtained and respected before an ad request; the app keeps ads disabled when the required consent setup is unavailable.

Android leaderboard data

Android version 1.3.0 uses Google Play Games Services for its leaderboards. If you use that service, Google Play Games processes your gamer identity (including your gamer name and profile avatar), game scores submitted to leaderboards, and service analytics and diagnostics. The app submits your best total score and the score recorded when you first reach the 2048 milestone on one, two, or three faces. The game does not read your email address or friends list. Google encrypts Play Games data in transit using HTTPS and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy; you can manage or delete Play Games data through your Play Games profile or Google Account. Android 1.3.0 does not use advertising or an advertising ID.

Sharing a score

If you choose Share, the device opens its system share sheet with your score text. You choose whether and where to send it. The selected app or service then handles the information under its own privacy policy; 2048³ does not send the score to a server.

Network access

On iOS, when ads are enabled, the device connects directly to the ad partners described above. On Android 1.3.0, the app can send leaderboard scores to Google Play Games over an encrypted connection. You can play locally without opening a leaderboard. The app also accesses network services when you choose to open a link or share content. Those services handle the resulting information under their own policies.

Children

2048³ is not directed to children, and Luna and Sol does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content and data use must follow the age rating and applicable child privacy rules.

Your choices and data deletion

You can decline the iOS tracking prompt and change that choice in iOS Settings. If a required consent screen is shown in a future release, you can use it to make or change the choices it describes. You can remove local gameplay data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Contact hello@lunaandsol.org with privacy questions. Ad partners may retain data they receive according to their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the app's data practices change. This version describes the planned ad-supported iOS 1.4.0 release and Android 1.3.0, which has Play Games leaderboards but no ads or Ad Free purchase. Check this page for later updates.
