2048³ for Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Applies to Android version 1.2.0

In short: this release has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, or online leaderboard. Gameplay data stays on your device.

Who we are

2048³ is published by Luna and Sol. For privacy questions, contact hello@lunaandsol.org.

Information the app handles

The app does not collect or send personal information to Luna and Sol. Your scores, best score, game state, and appearance preference are stored locally on your device. They remain there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

This release does not use advertising, analytics, account sign-in, or an online leaderboard. It does not request access to your location, contacts, or photos.

Sharing a score

If you choose Share, Android opens its system share sheet with your score text. You choose whether and where to send it. The selected app or service then handles the information under its own privacy policy; 2048³ does not send the score to a server.

Network access

The app does not transmit gameplay or personal data to Luna and Sol. Android's internet permission may be present for platform and link-handling functionality; opening a link or sharing content is initiated by you and may involve services governed by their own policies.

Children

2048³ does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

If a later version adds online services, advertising, analytics, or other data practices, this policy will be updated to describe them before that version is distributed.
