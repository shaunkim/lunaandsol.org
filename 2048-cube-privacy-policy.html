2048³ for iOS and Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

In short: Version 1.5.3 of 2048³ uses Google AdMob for a small banner and occasional ads between games. Google presents regional privacy choices where required. On iPhone and iPad, you can decline Apple’s tracking permission and continue playing. You may still see non-personalized ads. The one-time Ad Free purchase removes ads permanently.

Who we are

2048³ is published by Luna and Sol. For privacy questions, contact hello@lunaandsol.org.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, best score, game state, and appearance preference are stored locally on your device. The app does not send this gameplay information to Luna and Sol or use it to select ads. It remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

If you buy Ad Free, Apple or Google processes the payment through the store for your device. The app receives the purchase entitlement needed to remove ads and can check it again when you restore or recheck purchases. Luna and Sol does not receive your payment card or billing information.

Advertising and information used by ad partners

Version 1.5.3 uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) directly on iOS and Android. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks regional privacy choices before ads are requested. The SDK and Google’s ad services may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, crash logs, performance data, and other diagnostics. Google may use this information for ad delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention, and may share data with its advertising partners. See Google’s SDK data disclosures for iOS and Android.

The game does not send scores, moves, saved game state or other gameplay activity to ad partners for targeting, and Luna and Sol does not operate separate game-performance or user-behavior analytics. Google Play Games separately receives leaderboard scores and gamer identity when you use its optional leaderboard feature. Ad partners may associate ad events and technical data with a device or advertising identifier as described in their own privacy disclosures. Luna and Sol does not receive individual advertising identifiers or raw ad-request records through the app, though partners may provide aggregated advertising reports.

On iOS 1.5.3, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt appears when the game first becomes eligible to show ads, after any applicable Google consent form. If you allow tracking, Google may use the device’s advertising identifier to personalize and measure ads. If you decline or tracking permission is unavailable, the game requests non-personalized ads without sending that identifier, where Google’s privacy signals permit ad requests. Non-personalized ads can still involve ad delivery, fraud prevention, and aggregated measurement. Both platforms may skip ads if Google cannot establish the required privacy signals or supply an ad.

Android leaderboard data

Android uses Google Play Games Services for its leaderboards. If you use that service, Google Play Games processes your gamer identity (including your gamer name and profile avatar), game scores submitted to leaderboards, and service analytics and diagnostics. The app submits your best total score and the score recorded when you first reach the 2048 milestone on one, two, or three faces. The game does not read your email address or friends list. Google encrypts Play Games data in transit using HTTPS and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy; you can manage or delete Play Games data through your Play Games profile or Google Account.

Sharing a score

If you choose Share, the device opens its system share sheet with your score text. You choose whether and where to send it. The selected app or service then handles the information under its own privacy policy; 2048³ does not send the score to a server.

Network access

When regional privacy choices permit ad requests, version 1.5.3 connects to Google Mobile Ads and related Google ad services. Android can send leaderboard scores to Google Play Games, and iOS can send leaderboard scores and achievements to Apple Game Center. You can play locally without using those features. The app also accesses network services when you choose to open a link or share content. Those services handle the resulting information under their own policies.

Children

2048³ is not directed to children, and Luna and Sol does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content and data use must follow the age rating and applicable child privacy rules.

Your choices and data deletion

You can change your tracking permission under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can manage your advertising ID in Android privacy settings. On either platform, you can open Google Ad Privacy Options from the game menu where Google makes them available. Use Remove Ads to buy Ad Free or Restore Purchases to recover a previous purchase. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes locally stored gameplay data and preferences. The app has no Luna and Sol account or server for these features. Contact hello@lunaandsol.org with privacy questions.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The AdMob and tracking settings described above apply to version 1.5.3 as it becomes available. Older iOS builds that serve ads may use Unity LevelPlay and Meta Audience Network. Some older releases also have an in-app switch for ads. Those releases may process device identifiers, ad delivery and interaction information, and diagnostic information through their respective ad providers.
