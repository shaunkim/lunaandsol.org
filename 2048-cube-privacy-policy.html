2048³ for iOS and Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Applies to iOS 1.4.0 and Android 1.5.0

In short: iOS 1.4.0 uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with Meta Audience Network for a small bottom banner and occasional interstitial ads. Android 1.5.0 uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) directly for the same ad formats, with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable consent messages. You can turn ads off in the app or make a one-time Ad Free purchase through your device's store. Ads stay unavailable when required consent cannot be established. Android also uses Google Play Games for optional leaderboards.

Who we are

2048³ is published by Luna and Sol. For privacy questions, contact hello@lunaandsol.org.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, best score, game state, and appearance preference are stored locally on your device. The app does not send this gameplay information to Luna and Sol or use it to select ads. It remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

If you buy Ad Free, Apple or Google processes the payment through the store for your device. The app receives the purchase entitlement needed to remove ads and can check it again when you restore or recheck purchases. Luna and Sol does not receive your payment card or billing information.

Advertising and information used by ad partners

On iOS 1.4.0, Unity LevelPlay mediates ads from Meta Audience Network. On Android 1.5.0, the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK directly, without LevelPlay or Meta. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) obtains and presents consent choices configured for applicable regions before ads are requested. Google's SDK and ad services may process the device's IP address (which can be used to infer approximate location), advertising and device identifiers (including Android Advertising ID subject to the user's settings), ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic information. Google describes these data uses as including advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. The SDK may share data with Google and its advertising partners. See Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and UMP consent guidance for details. iOS ad partners may process device identifiers, ad delivery and interaction data, and technical or diagnostic information as described in their own privacy disclosures.

The game does not send scores, moves, saved game state or other gameplay activity to ad partners for targeting, and Luna and Sol does not operate separate game-performance or user-behavior analytics. Google Play Games separately receives leaderboard scores and gamer identity when you use its optional leaderboard feature. Ad partners may associate ad events and technical data with a device or advertising identifier as described in their own privacy disclosures. Luna and Sol does not receive individual advertising identifiers or raw ad-request records through the app, though partners may provide aggregated advertising reports.

On Android, ads are enabled by default for new installations, subject to applicable consent choices. You can switch ads off in Ad Privacy in the game menu; when ads are off, the app does not request or refresh ads. Google UMP presents consent choices when required and provides a privacy-options path where applicable. Your consent choices are applied before Google Mobile Ads is initialized. A local ad setting does not replace required regional consent. On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission controls access to the advertising identifier for tracking. You can decline or later change it in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads may be unavailable if required privacy signals cannot be applied.

Android leaderboard data

Android uses Google Play Games Services for its leaderboards. If you use that service, Google Play Games processes your gamer identity (including your gamer name and profile avatar), game scores submitted to leaderboards, and service analytics and diagnostics. The app submits your best total score and the score recorded when you first reach the 2048 milestone on one, two, or three faces. The game does not read your email address or friends list. Google encrypts Play Games data in transit using HTTPS and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy; you can manage or delete Play Games data through your Play Games profile or Google Account.

Sharing a score

If you choose Share, the device opens its system share sheet with your score text. You choose whether and where to send it. The selected app or service then handles the information under its own privacy policy; 2048³ does not send the score to a server.

Network access

When ads are enabled and consent allows them, Android connects to Google Mobile Ads and related Google ad services; iOS connects to the ad services described above. On Android, the app can also send leaderboard scores to Google Play Games over an encrypted connection. You can play locally without opening a leaderboard. The app also accesses network services when you choose to open a link or share content. Those services handle the resulting information under their own policies.

Children

2048³ is not directed to children, and Luna and Sol does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content and data use must follow the age rating and applicable child privacy rules.

Your choices and data deletion

You can decline the iOS tracking prompt and change that choice in iOS Settings. You can manage or reset Android's advertising ID in Android privacy settings, use Google UMP privacy options where available, or switch ads off in the game. You can also make a one-time Ad Free purchase through the store. You can remove locally stored choices and gameplay data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. The app does not provide an account-based deletion request because it does not operate an account or server for these features. Contact hello@lunaandsol.org with privacy questions. Ad partners handle information they receive under their own privacy disclosures.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the app's data practices change. This version describes iOS 1.4.0 and the planned ad-supported Android 1.5.0 release. Check this page for later updates.
