Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

In short: the listed apps do not require an account. 2048³ iOS 1.4.0 is planned to include ads from Unity LevelPlay and Meta Audience Network; its current Android 1.2.0 release does not include ads. Flame Aid's listed versions do not use advertising or analytics. See each app section for details.

  • 2048³ — iOS 1.4.0 (planned) and Android 1.2.0
  • Flame Aid — iOS 1.0 and Android 1.0

Who we are

Luna and Sol publishes the apps covered by this policy. For privacy questions, contact hello@lunaandsol.org.

2048³

2048³ scores, best score, game state, and appearance preference are stored locally on your device; the app does not send gameplay data to Luna and Sol or use it to select ads. iOS 1.4.0 is planned to show banner and occasional interstitial ads through Unity LevelPlay and Meta Audience Network, with a one-time Ad Free purchase. Their SDKs may process device identifiers (including the advertising identifier only when Apple permits access), advertising data such as ad delivery, impressions and taps, and limited technical data needed to deliver, measure, secure and troubleshoot ads. This includes ad-service performance data and SDK crash or diagnostic reports. The game does not send scores, moves, saved game state or other gameplay activity for ad targeting, and Luna and Sol does not run separate game-performance or user-behavior analytics. Meta's SDK privacy manifest declares device identifiers and advertising data for third-party advertising and tracking, and unlinked crash and diagnostic data for analytics. Unity's LevelPlay privacy questionnaire identifies device identifiers for tracking, advertising data and performance data for advertising, and product-interaction data when mediation partners share it. Ad partners may associate ad events and technical data with a device or advertising identifier as described in their own privacy disclosures. Luna and Sol does not receive individual advertising identifiers or raw ad-request records through the app, though partners may provide aggregated advertising reports. Apple's App Tracking Transparency controls access to the advertising identifier for tracking on iOS; declining may limit ad availability. Required regional consent must be obtained before ad requests, and the app keeps ads disabled if its consent setup is unavailable. Android 1.2.0 does not include ads or an Ad Free purchase.

Flame Aid

Flame Aid performs combustion calculations on your device. Fuel selections, calculation inputs and results, app preferences, and the calculation log are stored locally in the app. The log can contain up to 1,000 rows; you can remove records or clear the log in the app. The listed versions do not send this information to Luna and Sol, require an account, use advertising, or use analytics. The Android version does not request internet access.

Sharing, copying, and exporting

If you choose to share a 2048³ score or export Flame Aid results, your device opens system sharing options. Flame Aid for Android can also copy CSV text to your device's clipboard when you choose Copy. You decide whether and where to send or paste this information. The selected app or service handles it under its own privacy policy; Luna and Sol does not receive the content through these actions.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and keep it only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and maintain related records. Support messages are handled through the email services involved in sending and receiving them. Do not include sensitive personal information in a support email.

Data sharing, security, and deletion

The apps do not send gameplay or calculation data to Luna and Sol. For 2048³ iOS 1.4.0 advertising, data may be shared directly with Unity LevelPlay and Meta Audience Network as described above. If you choose to share or copy content, the selected app or service receives it. Because gameplay and calculation data is stored on your device, you can delete it by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Flame Aid also lets you clear its calculation log in the app. Device backups and operating-system services are controlled by your device provider and your settings.

Luna and Sol does not operate an account system or server for these app features. Protect your device with the security features provided by its operating system.

Children's privacy

The apps covered by this policy do not require users to provide personal information or knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy applies to the apps and versions listed above. Before a new app or app version introduces different data practices, this policy will be updated with details for that app or version. We will revise the date above when the policy changes. Please check this page periodically.
