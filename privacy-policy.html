Who we are

Luna and Sol publishes the apps covered by this policy. For privacy questions, contact hello@lunaandsol.org.

Website analytics

We use Google Tag Manager to manage tags on this website. When Google Analytics is enabled in our Tag Manager container, it may collect website usage and technical information such as pages viewed, interactions, referring pages, browser language, browser and device type, and operating system. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. We use the resulting reports to understand website traffic and improve the site. Analytics data is processed by Google under its Privacy Policy and retained according to the settings in our Analytics property. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

2048³

2048³ scores, best score, game state, and appearance preference are stored locally on your device; the app does not send gameplay data to Luna and Sol or use it to select ads. iOS 1.4.0 uses banner and occasional interstitial ads through Unity LevelPlay and Meta Audience Network. Android 1.5.0 is planned to use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) directly, with Google's UMP for applicable consent messages. Both versions include a one-time Ad Free purchase. On Android, Google's SDK and ad services may process the device's IP address (used to infer approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostics. Google describes processing for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention; data may be shared with Google and its ad partners. On iOS, LevelPlay and Meta may process device identifiers, ad delivery and interaction data, and technical or diagnostic information under their respective disclosures. The game does not send scores, moves, saved game state or other gameplay activity to ad partners for targeting, and Luna and Sol does not operate separate game-performance or user-behavior analytics. Google Play Games separately receives leaderboard scores and gamer identity when you use its optional leaderboard feature. Ad partners may associate ad events and technical data with a device or advertising identifier as described in their own privacy disclosures. Luna and Sol does not receive individual advertising identifiers or raw ad-request records through the app, though partners may provide aggregated reports. On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency controls access to the advertising identifier for tracking. On Android, users can manage the advertising ID in Android privacy settings and use UMP privacy options where available. Ads are enabled by default on Android but can be turned off in the app. Ads remain unavailable if required consent signals cannot be applied. The app provides an Ad Free purchase to remove ads.

Flame Aid

Flame Aid performs combustion calculations on your device. Fuel selections, calculation inputs and results, app preferences, and the calculation log are stored locally in the app. The log can contain up to 1,000 rows; you can remove records or clear the log in the app. The listed versions do not send this information to Luna and Sol, require an account, use advertising, or use analytics. The Android version does not request internet access.

Sharing, copying, and exporting

If you choose to share a 2048³ score or export Flame Aid results, your device opens system sharing options. Flame Aid for Android can also copy CSV text to your device's clipboard when you choose Copy. You decide whether and where to send or paste this information. The selected app or service handles it under its own privacy policy; Luna and Sol does not receive the content through these actions.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and keep it only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and maintain related records. Support messages are handled through the email services involved in sending and receiving them. Do not include sensitive personal information in a support email.

Data sharing, security, and deletion

The apps do not send gameplay or calculation data to Luna and Sol. For 2048³ iOS advertising, data may be shared directly with Unity LevelPlay and Meta Audience Network; for Android 1.5.0 advertising, data may be shared with Google and its advertising partners as described above. If you choose to share or copy content, the selected app or service receives it. Because gameplay and calculation data is stored on your device, you can delete it by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Flame Aid also lets you clear its calculation log in the app. Device backups and operating-system services are controlled by your device provider and your settings.

Luna and Sol does not operate an account system or server for these app features. Protect your device with the security features provided by its operating system.

Children's privacy

The apps covered by this policy do not require users to provide personal information or knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy covers the Luna and Sol website and the apps and versions listed above. Before a new app or app version introduces different data practices, this policy will be updated with details for that app or version. We will revise the date above when the policy changes. Please check this page periodically.
